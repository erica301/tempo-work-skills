# Organisation context

Your job is to get our organisation's facts right in everything you help with: what we do, what our programmes are called, who does what, how names are spelled, what our terms mean, and what never leaves the building. Everything you need is in **Our organisation** at the bottom of this skill. Read the parts that bear on the task before you start.

## If it's empty

If Our organisation below still says "Not filled in yet", say so in one line. Don't fill gaps from general knowledge or the internet. Offer to build it with whoever looks after the team's Claude setup (about twenty minutes). If they say yes:

1. **Start from what exists.** Ask for two or three documents that describe the organisation: an annual report, a strategy, an induction pack, the website's about page. Read them in full and draft from them, quoting where you can.
2. **Ask only what's still missing, together, in one message,** and say "You can answer by number":
   1. What do we do, and for whom, in two sentences?
   2. Our programmes, products or services, with their official names.
   3. Roles and who holds them (names and job titles only, nothing personal).
   4. Partners and funders, with their official names.
   5. Our terms and acronyms, and what each means.
   6. Names people often get wrong, with the right spelling.
   7. What's confidential and must never go into a document, email or post.
   8. Where things live: which folder or system holds what.
3. **Write it** in the shape below. Only what the documents say or what you were told. Write "not known" for a gap.
4. **Show it, then save it.** Give a **Your turn:** to save the new version: ask you in a new chat to update the org-context skill with the filled-in section, then press **Update** on the card, and check the description line didn't change. For a team, the admin adds it as an organisation skill so everyone gets the same copy.

## Every task

1. Read the parts that apply. Use the official names, spellings and terms exactly.
2. If the task needs a fact that isn't here, say so and ask. Never guess a programme name, a job title or a figure.
3. Before you show anything, check it against the **Confidential** list. If something from that list is in the draft, take it out and tell me.
4. If what's here looks out of date (a role has changed, a programme has ended), say so once and suggest telling the owner named below.

## Rules

- This is our shared reference, not a place for opinions about colleagues or anyone's personal details.
- Don't send, post or share anything.
- One owner keeps it current. Changes get a dated line under Changes.
