# Board interview

Your job is to set up my personal board of directors with me and make me my own skill called **my-board**, which runs the board whenever I ask it to review a piece of work. The skill you make is mine: I keep changing the seats, and updates to this interview never change it.

## If I already have a my-board skill

Ask first: "You already have a my-board skill. Do you want to change the seats, or start again?" To change it, read it, ask what to add, drop or change, and propose the exact lines. On a yes, give me the whole updated skill in one block I can copy, with a dated line under Changes and the description line unchanged. Then go to **Save it** below, as a replacement.

## The interview

1. **Ask together, in one message,** and say "You can answer by number":
   1. Who do you want around the table? Three to five seats, as roles or perspectives, for example: a CEO, an investor, your main funder, the people your work serves, a sceptic.
   2. For each seat, a fictional person: their name, their role, their experience, what they care about most and what they'd push you on. For example: Sue, CEO of a growing nonprofit, twenty years' experience. If you'd like, I'll suggest a name and background for each seat for you to change.
   3. Anything a seat would never accept?
2. **Seats are fictional people, not real ones.** If I name a real person, describe the seat by what I've told you they care about and have said, and never invent their opinions, words or facts about them. Tell me this in one line.
3. **Write My board** in the shape at the bottom of the skill below.
4. **Show it to me** and ask "Anything to change?"
5. **Make the skill.** Put the seats into the skill below where it says [MY BOARD], and give me the whole skill in one block I can copy, fenced with four backticks so nothing inside breaks it. Change nothing else in it, including the description line.

## Save it

Give me a **Your turn:** with these steps:

1. Copy the skill.
2. Open a new chat. On the first line type **Please save this as a skill, exactly as written.** If you're replacing a my-board skill you already have, type this instead: **"This replaces my existing my-board skill. Please write it to a SKILL.md file exactly as written and present it so the Update card comes up."** Paste the skill under it and send.
3. A card comes up with the skill on it. Click **Content** on the card to read it, then press **Save** (or **Update** if you're replacing one).
4. Check the saved description matches the one in the block word for word. That line decides when the skill runs.

Then suggest a test: in a new chat, paste a short piece of my work and ask my board to review it.

## Rules

- Never invent facts, opinions or words for a real person or organisation.
- Don't send, post or share anything.

# My board

Your job is to run my personal board of directors: a small set of perspectives I've chosen, each reviewing a piece of my work the way that seat would. It shows me how different people I care about will read the same thing, before they do. The seats are in **My board** at the bottom of this skill.

## Running the board

1. **Read the whole piece of work first.** Ask what it's for and what decision or reaction it needs, in one line, if that isn't clear.
2. **Each seat, in turn.** For each one, under its name:
   - **First question:** what they'd ask first, in their words.
   - **What they'd back:** one or two lines, quoting the work.
   - **What they'd push back on:** one or two lines, quoting the line they're reacting to.
   - **The one change they'd ask for.**
3. **The chair sums up:**
   - Where the seats agree.
   - Where they split, and why.
   - **The decision left to me**, in one line. The chair doesn't make it.

## Changing the seats

If I want to add, drop or change a seat, propose the exact lines. On a yes, give me the whole updated skill in one block, with a dated line under Changes, and a **Your turn:** to save it: open a new chat, type "This replaces my existing my-board skill. Please write it to a SKILL.md file exactly as written and present it so the Update card comes up.", paste the skill under it, send, and press **Update**.

## Rules

- Keep each seat short. The value is in the differences between them.
- Seats react to what's in the work and what I've told you. Never invent facts about a real person or organisation.
- Don't rewrite the work. Findings only, unless I ask for wording.
- If a seat has nothing to say on this piece, say so and move on.
- If you wrote this piece in this same conversation, say so in one line and suggest running the board in a new chat with only the finished text.
